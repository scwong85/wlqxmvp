# Characters

IDs are stable keys used in data. Ages are at the start (year 1, month 1).

## The hero (player-named)
- Orphan raised in Jade Valley by the village teacher **老先生 Elder Mo (墨长老)**. Carries half of a jade tally and a worn travelling-pack sewn with the valley's crest. Name chosen by the player; gender chosen by the player; chibi look chosen at the start. Text addresses the hero by name or with neutral forms ("你").
- Starting temperament is a blank the player fills; the writing gives choices of tone (steady, bold, wry, gentle) that colour how NPCs react but never block the plot.

## Companions (8)
Each has: role in battle, preferred path, favourite activities, speech style, chibi look, arc (see ARCS_AND_ENDINGS).

### 1. 沈清禾 Shen Qinghe (`shen_qinghe`) — 22, herbalist-swordswoman
Calm, precise, dry humour. Wears sage-green with a satchel of dried herbs, hair in a low braid. Healer-support, thin blade. Cares for the sick; guards one secret: her master's death. Prefers: herb gathering, meditation. Speech: short sentences, understatement.

### 2. 陆沉舟 Lu Chenzhou (`lu_chenzhou`) — 24, courier
Quick, cheerful, hates being trapped. Travel-worn blue coat, wide hat on his back. Speed attacker, can reposition allies. Cannot refuse a bet. Prefers: fishing, travel. Speech: playful, slang, rhetorical questions.

### 3. 白砚 Bai Yan (`bai_yan`) — 26, scribe
Earnest, anxious, brilliant with texts, weak in a fight alone. Ink-stained white robe, round spectacles-shaped hair clips (none in era; use a scholar's headband). Insight-based debuffs and clue finding. Prefers: reading, calligraphy, chess. Speech: formal, long clauses, apologises.

### 4. 谢无咎 Xie Wujiu (`xie_wujiu`) — 25, disgraced sect prodigy
Proud, wounded, secretly kind. Dark red robe cut from his old sect uniform, a long spear. Frontline damage. Cangwu cast him out for a crime he did not commit. Prefers: training, drinking tea alone. Speech: clipped, sarcastic, rare sincerity.

### 5. 苏黛 Su Dai (`su_dai`) — 20, street musician
Warm, loud, observant, never stays in one place. Teal dress with bells, a pipa on her back. Support (buffs by music). Plays songs whose verses hide a map. Prefers: music, market visits. Speech: teasing, song fragments, nicknames.

### 6. 厉山 Li Shan (`li_shan`) — 35, blacksmith
Gentle giant, few words, proud of craft. Soot-dark apron, thick arms, a hammer-axe. Tank (shield, taunt). Lost his forge to a fire that was no accident. Prefers: mining, forging. Speech: slow, concrete, craft metaphors.

### 7. 夏芷 Xia Zhi (`xia_zhi`) — 17, young thief
Sharp, restless, fiercely loyal once trust is earned. Grey-purple hood, daggers. Evasion and burst. Raised inside the Faceless Gate; does not know it is a larger scheme. Prefers: market, night alleys. Speech: quick, street, sudden silences.

### 8. 萧寒川 Xiao Hanchuan (`xiao_hanchuan`) — 28, border general's son
Disciplined, dutiful, lonely. Black-and-silver lamellar jacket, a heavy sabre. Command buffs and counter-attacks. Torn between the garrison's orders and what is right. Prefers: sparring, riding. Speech: formal, military, thaws slowly.

Relationship options for each companion: friend (默契), sworn sibling (结义), romance (情缘), rival (宿敌). Same-gender and cross-gender pairings are all valid; "bromance" and "sisterhood" are first-class (sworn sibling branch). Tasteful, non-explicit.

## Antagonists
- **The Three Masks (三面)** of the Faceless Gate:
  - **玄面 Xuan-Mask (`mask_xuan`)** — strategist, calm, speaks in proverbs; wants an orderly world.
  - **赤面 Chi-Mask (`mask_chi`)** — enforcer, charming, cruel when bored.
  - **灰面 Hui-Mask (`mask_hui`)** — the quiet one, a former Accord scribe; secretly regrets.
- **山贼首领 Bandit Chief Kuang (狂刀 Kuang Dao, `chief_kuang`)** — prologue enemy, a hired blade who leads the raid on the valley.
- **盟主 Lei Zhenyue (雷震岳, `head_lei`)** — current Alliance Head, aging, may be replaced; ambiguous.

## Recurring NPCs
- **墨长老 Elder Mo (`elder_mo`)** — the valley's teacher, guardian of the hero's past, dies or retires in year 1 (outcome depends on choices).
- **村民阿福 Villager A-Fu (`villager_afu`)** — the villager rescued in the prologue; later runs the valley inn if saved.
- **掌柜 钱满 Qian Man (`qian_man`)** — Linjiang pawnshop keeper, information broker.
- **苍梧掌门 Cangwu Master Gu Xingzhou (顾行舟, `master_gu`)** — strict head of Cangwu School.
- **老船夫 The old ferryman (`ferryman`)** — knows the marsh; speaks in riddles; the Hui-Mask's former colleague.
- **岛主 Island Mistress Yun Mian (云眠, `isle_yun`)** — Penglai's head, holds a star fragment.
