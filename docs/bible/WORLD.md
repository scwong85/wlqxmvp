# World

## Setting
**青岚州 (Qinglan Province)**, 景昭 era. A river-and-mountain province of towns, trade roads and sects, loosely held by a distant court that leaves the martial world (江湖) to govern itself. Technology: swords, spears, sabres, bows, horse and boat, herbal medicine, printing. No magic in the fantasy sense; extraordinary skill is the cultivation of body, breath and mind (内功), which is grounded and costly.

## The Jade Accord (玉契)
Two centuries ago a war between sects nearly emptied the province. The survivors carved the **玉契**: a jade tablet split into nine parts, kept by nine houses, and a rule that every three years the houses meet on the **论剑台** (Sword-Debate Platform) at 苍梧山 and choose a **盟主** (Alliance Head) who keeps the peace. Disputes go to the gathering instead of the battlefield. The system has frayed: some houses are corrupt, some have no heirs, one has been quietly replaced.

## The Nine-Star Manual (九曜残卷)
A lost manual attributed to the founder of the Accord, split into nine fragments (the *stars*). Whoever joins all nine learns how to **re-forge the Accord**: to rewrite who holds power. Eight fragments are hidden in ruins, houses and relics. The ninth is kept in a vault under the 沉星泽 marsh, opened only by a **jade tally** (the hero carries half of one).

## The Faceless Gate (无相门)
A hidden group that wants the manual to remake the order. It works through patient pressure: bandit probes, bought officials, planted heirs, rumours. Its members wear no marks. Leaders: **the Three Masks** (see CHARACTERS). Not purely villainous: its founders saw the Accord decay, and some members are decent. This ambiguity drives the "Shadow" ending and Xia Zhi's arc.

## Martial arts
- **Paths (三道):** 玄拳 Mystic-Flow Fist (power, body), 青桐剑 Green-Paulownia Sword (agility, insight), 听雨法 Listening-Rain Method (spirit, insight).
- **Realms (境界):** 初窥门径, 登堂入室, 小有所成, 融会贯通, 炉火纯青, 返璞归真, 宗师 (seven steps; details in the M20 design).
- **Techniques (招式)** are learned from manuals (秘籍) and teachers; **life skills** (钓鱼, 采矿, 医术, 音律, 棋艺, 厨艺) give small passive benefits.
- Skill is slow: no overnight power. Power in the story comes from time, teachers, trials and companions.

## The nine houses (holders of the tablet parts)
1. 苍梧派 Cangwu School (orthodox, strict)  2. 临江漕帮 Linjiang Barge Guild (trade)  3. 青衣楼 Green-Robe Pavilion (informants, scribes)  4. 铁山堡 Iron-Hill Fort (smiths, soldiers)  5. 雁门军 Yanmen Garrison (border army)  6. 蓬岛隐宗 Penglai Hermits (aloof)  7. 听雨阁 Listening-Rain Hall (healers and musicians)  8. 白鹭书院 White-Egret Academy (scholars)  9. **玉谷** (a house in name only: the hero's lost valley lineage; the Faceless Gate believes it is extinct)

The hero's valley lineage is the ninth house. This is revealed gradually (see TIMELINE) and is why the hero's tally half matters.

## Factions the player can side with (existing prologue flags)
Pavilion (青衣楼), Couriers (镖局 / escort bureau), Scribes (书斋 / White-Egret), Neutral. They persist as standing values across the campaign.
