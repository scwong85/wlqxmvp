## Companion stage 4 (the resolution and the relationship scene)
Stage 4 is one scene per companion plus a short follow-up. It needs stage 3 done (or closed as lost, which gives a shorter farewell scene), and a bond of 16. It records the relationship: **friend / sworn sibling / romance / rival**, chosen from the options the bond and earlier choices allow. Tasteful, non-explicit. The relationship becomes a flag (`bond_friend` etc.), which the epilogue (M26) reads.
| Companion | Weeks | Stage 4 sets |
|---|---|---|
| Shen Qinghe | 124-138 | healer-head, border healer or shadow, plus the relationship |
| Lu Chenzhou | 126-140 | the bureau reformed, the road alone or sold |
| Bai Yan | 130-145 | the true history, the academy or the Gate's tool |
| Xie Wujiu | 124-136 | a school with the hero, a teacher's seat or the rival champion |
| Su Dai | 138-150 | the song as the ninth fragment's key, the road, or stolen |
| Li Shan | 134-146 | master smith, retired forge or the Gate's smith |
| Xia Zhi | 128-140 | the Breaker, disappeared or the Gate's lieutenant |
| Xiao Hanchuan | 140-152 | garrison head, wanderer or dead at the pass |
A companion whose arc is `fallen` can appear once more as a boss or a rescue target (a chosen scene, never permadeath in battle).

All five big endings are reachable (conditions are in ARCS_AND_ENDINGS). If the hero never reaches the Platform (hard lock is impossible: the finale triggers at week 171 either way), the fallback ending is "The Long Road".

## Totals for the year
- About 35 fixed scenes (about 20 main plus the 8 stage-4 scenes and follow-ups), 30 random templates, 13 battles.
- 5 new areas (3 vault, 2 platform), 3 new backgrounds, the Masks as boss units, 2 new music themes.
- All 8 arcs reach 4/4 (or a farewell for a lost arc); the relationship branch is recorded for each.
- Power curve: realm 5 at week 121; realm 6 around week 165 for a steady player (a half-story player at about 175; the finale is not gated by realm, only the "pure" endings are).
