# Year 2 outline: "Cracks" (M24 draft for your approval)

Weeks 61 to 120 (year 2 = 12 months of 5 weeks). A "scene" is a fixed event (dialogue, sometimes a choice or a battle). Year 1 content stays as built. Defaults are marked **(default)**; reply "go" to accept them all.

## Shape of the year
| Quarter | Weeks | Place | Story job | New | Power target (steady player) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q5 The Undertow Surfaces | 61-75 | Linjiang + valley | The Faceless Gate shows itself; a house is replaced; Elder Mo's fork pays off | Xia Zhi joins (wk 66) | Realm 3, duo tech for every companion |

Gating rule is the same as year 1: story scenes ask for a realm, never one specific attribute. A player who skips an optional scene never blocks the plot (missed scenes change flags only).

## Totals for the year
- About 40 fixed scenes (about 25 main plus the 24 arc stages), 30 random templates, 14 battles.
- 7 new areas (4 Yanmen, 3 Penglai), 2 new companions with full data, looks, duo techniques, 5 new backgrounds.
- 22 arc stage events (stages 2 and 3 for 6 companions, 1 to 3 for Xia and Xiao).
- Minigames: music and mining get one scored event each; fishing at the harbour.
- Power curve: realm 3 at wk 61 to realm 5 around wk 115 for a steady player.
