# Year 1 outline (M22 draft for your approval)

Weeks are absolute (1 to 60). Year 1 = 12 months of 5 weeks. A "scene" is a fixed event (a dialogue, sometimes with a battle or choice). Existing content is marked (done).

## Shape of the year
| Quarter | Weeks | Place | Story job | New companions | Power target (steady player) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 Winds over Jade Valley | 1-15 | 玉谷 | Prologue aftermath; the raid was a probe; Elder Mo gives the tally | Shen, Lu, Bai already met; their stage 1 arcs (done) | Realm 1, first technique, level 3 |
| Q2 Storm in Linjiang | 16-30 | 临江城 | Trade city, guilds; trace the first star fragment to the Barge Guild | Su Dai (wk 18), Li Shan (wk 24) | Realm 2, first manual, first forged gear |
| Q3 The Cangwu Gate | 31-45 | 苍梧山 | Sect tournament; Xie Wujiu's frame; first sight of the Hui-Mask | Xie Wujiu (wk 38) | Realm 3, a skill-tree branch, duo technique |
| Q4 First into the Marsh | 46-60 | 沉星泽 | The ferryman, the first vault gate, the year-end ambush and Elder Mo fork | none (all six met) | Realm 3 to 4, 3-person party |

Gating rule: story scenes ask for a realm (Q2 start: realm 1, Q3 tournament: realm 2, marsh: realm 3), never for one specific attribute, so any path can play the year.

## Elder Mo fork (week 59-60)
Based on three things the player did during the year: the tally scene (kept the secret or shared it), whether the valley was protected at the prologue (villager_saved) and the ambush result.
| Outcome | Condition | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Elder Mo lives and stays | ambush won, villager saved | he guides the valley from home; hometown flag `valley_kept` |
| Elder Mo retires with the hero | ambush won, villager lost | travels with the hero as an advisor line (no battles) |
| Elder Mo falls | ambush lost | the hero inherits his seal; the valley's fate is decided in year 2 |
The game continues into year 2 either way.
