# M20b Report - Arts and gear

- Skill trees: 8 nodes per school (24 total). Stat nodes and technique nodes; each school ends in a signature art. Points: 2 per realm plus up to 8 from path progress.
- Manuals: 6 items (3 exclusive kinds, two of them gated by realm). One manual per kind can be studied; studying costs 4 stamina and a slot. Found in the world (area wanders) and as battle rewards.
- Equipment: 9 pieces (weapon/armor/charm, 3 tiers), forged from ore and coins, one worn per slot.
- Battle: tree and gear bonuses (hp, body, power, agility, spirit, tp, resolve) apply to the hero.
- Screen: Cultivation sheet > "Arts" opens the Arts screen with three tabs.
- Save: cultivation.nodes, manual_choices, equipment (older saves load empty).
- Tests: 2072 unit checks, smoke covers sheet and arts layouts at 4 sizes x 2 languages.
